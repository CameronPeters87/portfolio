## Open the app

Open `index.html` (double-click it, or use the live link once published). It runs on your

you're weak on. That's your daily home base.

## The daily ritual (this is the whole thing)

Every study day, in order, ~60–90 min on a work day, 2–3 hrs on a day off:

1. **Warm up (5 min):** open the app → tap **Quiz → Drill weak areas**. 5 questions on
   what you got wrong before. This is the part that makes it stick.
2. **Learn (main block):** watch today's **Udemy** videos (the app/plan tells you which),
   or do the week's **lab**. Read the matching **cheat sheet** after.
3. **Prove it (10 min):** app → **Quiz → today's domain**, 5 questions. Then tell Kiro
   **"log my misses and update my tracker."**

That's it. Three steps. Don't overthink it.
